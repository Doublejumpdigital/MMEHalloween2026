Daily notes

Written each morning with the data refresh

Definitions. Revenue, orders, sessions, conversion rate and AOV come from Shopify and are the source of truth. Google and Meta figures are platform-reported: useful for steering each channel, but both platforms claim credit for some of the same orders, so they don't sum to actual revenue. MER (marketing efficiency ratio) is Shopify revenue divided by total ad spend across both platforms.

Attribution lag. Platform-reported conversions for the most recent 2–3 days always restate upward as Google and Meta finish attributing. A soft platform ROAS inside that window is usually the lag, not the media.

Net sales & profit. Net sales is Shopify revenue after discounts and returns. The profit lens, when shown, is an estimate built on the client's blended gross margin; Shopify holds no per-item cost data for this store, so true gross profit isn't reportable until costs are entered (and would apply this season onward only).
